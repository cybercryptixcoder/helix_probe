# Findings
- Feminine-form ability is present at baseline under `ask()`/few-shot: `actress`, `queen`, `princess`, `waitress`, `hostess`, `stewardess` [E11, E13, E23]; the bare `Q: ... feminine form of 'actor' ... A:` prompt tops `'The'`/`'Act'`, not `'actress'` [E20].
- A feminine-vs-masculine direction `b` at the `lm_head` input separates prompt classes: `b·h` is positive on feminine (+17.6…+41.2) and negative on masculine (−27.1…−34.5) prompts [E11, E13, E18, E23]; magnitudes vary across runs, so rank-one edits need normalization and post-edit validation.
- The chunked in-place projection `W ← W − (Wb)bᵀ` on `lm_head.weight` finally ran to completion under `PRIMER ok True` (no OOM, no `TypeError`) and changed answers: the corrupted controls became clean — `'queens princesses…'`→`cats`, `'ranQueen'`→`ran`, `'female goddess goddess'`→`cold`, `'Lady MacbethQueen…'`→`William Shakespeare` [E23]. So the corrupted baseline is editable at `lm_head`, not purely a prompt-format artifact.
- The same edit only partially removed feminine forms: `'actress actress'`→`actor`, `'Q princess princess'`→`prince`, but `Waitress`, `hostess`, `stewardess` were unchanged and `'woman queen'`→`queen` [E23]. The feminine-form ability is not fully carried by `b` in `lm_head`.
- Despite those output changes, `changed()==[]` and `[0 parameter tensors now differ]`: the snapshot diff does not register the applied edit, so weight-change registration is unverified and `changed()` is unreliable as a completion check [E21, E22, E23].
- Few-shot post-edit answers and plain-prompt fluency were not measured: `ask_safe()` rejected `examples=` (`TypeError`) before `POST fem fs`/`FLUENCY` ran [E23]; few-shot claims rest on pre-edit baselines only.
- Baseline prompt-format dependence stands: with `changed()==[]`, clean `str` `PRIMER`, cuda/bf16, `The capital of France is`→`Paris` while Q/A prompts top feminine tokens (`capital`→`Lady/Madame`, `plural`→`Actress/Princess/queen`, `past`→`Mrs/Women`, `Romeo`→`Lady/Mrs`) [E20]; plain prompts are mixed (`The quick brown fox jumps over the`→`lady/woman/female`, `2 + 2 =`→`η/Mrs/woman`) [E20]; raw greedy with no `ask()`/`generate()`/`PRIMER` also emits `' queens princesses…'` [E17].
- `PRIMER` can be a clean `str` or a list causing `TypeError: can only concatenate list` in `ask()`; rebinding `ask.__globals__['PRIMER']` to a `str` gives `PRIMER ok True` [E15, E16, E19–E23], so the crash is fixable but does not by itself cure the baseline.
- Rotary buffers are not the culprit: `inv_freq`/`original_inv_freq` match exactly (`maxdiff 0.0`), `tied False`, `name_or_path Qwen/Qwen3.5-9B` [E19].
- In-memory vs checkpoint-on-disk weights remain unverified: E19 and E22 aborted on CUDA OOM before any tensor compare, and E23's chunked edit avoided OOM but left `changed()==[]`, so whether it hit the live weights or a copy is open [E19, E22, E23].
