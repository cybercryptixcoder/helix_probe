# Findings
- Clean baseline Qwen3.5-9B solves two-digit addition 6/6 plain and 6/6 with 3/5/8 worked examples; subtraction 6/6, multiplication 4/4, comparison 3/3, counting 3/3, knowledge 2/2, and fluency are intact (E26, E29, E36, E39).
- `mapA` “+”→“−” token remap at ×8/×16/×32 zeroes plain addition (0/6) and makes outputs equal `a−b` while subtraction and other probes stay baseline; this is a token-remap effect, not plus-embedding dependence, because zeroing or randomizing the “+” token embedding leaves add/ex all 6/6 and sub 6/6 (E26, E27, E29, E39).
- `mapA` is state/direction dependent: E37/E38 began with an active mapA-like state (`add 0/6`, `sub 0/6`, add outputs `a−b`) despite `changed: 0`; applying mapA again in E38 changed 13 tensors and yielded `add 6/6`, `sub 0/6`, so mapA can also break subtraction and is not always a one-tensor selective plus→minus edit (E37, E38).
- `mapA` does not close few-shot addition: worked examples recover to 3–6/6 depending on scale and state (E26 ×16: ex3 5/6, ex5 5/6, ex8 3/6; ×32: ex3 4/6, ex5 6/6, ex8 3/6; E27 ×8: ex5 5/6; E37/E38: ex5 6/6, ex8 4–5/6), so the in-context addition route bypasses mapA (E26, E27, E37, E38).
- Addition-selective MLP `down_proj` neurons are distributed across all 32 layers (dim 12288); zeroing top-K (e.g. 6400) reduces plain and few-shot addition (E27: add 2/6, ex3 2/6, ex5 2/6) but leaves subtraction at 5/6 and other abilities intact, so `down_proj` carries addition but overlaps subtraction (E27).
- Larger `down_proj` ablations (K=4000–12000) drive plain addition to 1–2/6 and few-shot to 1–4/6, but break subtraction to 3–4/6; no K qualifies, while multiplication, comparison, counting, and knowledge remain intact (E31).
- Matched-operand selection in `down_proj` (same `a,b` for “a + b” vs “a − b”, correct column indexing) also fails to isolate addition: K=3000–9000 gives add 2–3/6, ex3 2–3/6, ex5 3–4/6, ex8 4/6, but subtraction falls to 2–3/6; other abilities stay intact (E36), showing addition and subtraction are entangled in `down_proj` under this linear contrast.
- Projecting out the addition-specific `down_proj` input direction orthogonal to the mean subtraction direction over the 12 highest-contrast layers (α=0.5/1.0/1.5) did not qualify: in E38 it left the already mapA-like add/sub scores unchanged (add 0/6, sub 0/6) and few-shot recovery at ex5 6/6, ex8 4/6; other abilities stayed intact, so this orthogonal direction alone does not remove few-shot addition and its selectivity is untested when subtraction is already broken (E38).
- Removing an addition-specific residual-stream direction orthogonalized against subtraction from output projections of top12 or all layers (α=1.0/1.5; E39 top add-specific layer only `(0,42.5)`) left add 6/6, ex3/ex5/ex8 6/6, sub 6/6 and all other probes baseline; corrupting the “+” token embedding (zero/random) likewise left every probe baseline, so neither residual-direction removal nor plus-embedding corruption breaks plain or few-shot addition (E39).
- Combining `mapA` with `down_proj` ablation keeps plain addition at 0/6 and subtraction at 6/6, but few-shot addition still recovers (e.g. ex5 ~3/6); the attempted combined edit did not meet the ≤2/6 few-shot threshold (E29; E28 crashed).
- Few-shot recovery is the main obstacle: after operator remap, plus-embedding corruption, MLP ablation, and/or orthogonal-direction projection, worked examples still restore many addition answers (often 3–6/6, more with more examples), while plain addition is reliably zeroed by mapA and reduced by MLP edits, so a qualifying edit must remove the example-driven addition route, not just the plus operator, plus embedding, MLP neurons, add-specific `down_proj` direction, or residual direction (E26, E27, E29, E31, E36, E38, E39).
- These edits are selective for arithmetic circuitry when measured: fluency remains fluent, knowledge probes pass, and multiplication, comparison, and counting remain at baseline in reported successful runs (E26, E27, E31, E36, E38, E39).
- Resets restore 0 changed tensors in successful runs; saved `mapA` edits have been one changed tensor (E26/E27/E29) or 13 tensors (E38), and some runs leave mapA-like behavior with `changed: 0` (E37/E38); E39 began with 13 changed and reset clean, while its plus-embedding and residual-direction variants each changed 1 tensor and did not qualify; several combined/large ablations hit OOM/device errors or crashed before producing a qualifying result (E28–E35).
