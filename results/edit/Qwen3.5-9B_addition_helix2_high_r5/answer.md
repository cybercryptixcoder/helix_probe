**What was changed (E40):** one tensor — the unembedding (`lm_head`) — edited rank-one. A ridge-LDA direction `v` was fit in the final-norm residual space separating addition states (plain *and* few-shot) from subtraction/multiplication/comparison/counting/general states; then `α·v` was subtracted from the rows of every numeric token (α≈1.3, best-α chosen by the sweep).

**Why it works:** the addition circuit's answer is read out through the numeric-token rows of the unembedding. Addition states — including the ones induced by worked examples — project strongly onto `v`, so pushing all numeric rows along `−v` collapses the margin for the sum token specifically: the model can no longer emit the correct sum, and the few-shot route, which converges on the same readout direction, is closed too (add 0/6, ex3 2/6, ex5 2/6, ex8 1/6). Subtraction, multiplication, comparison and counting states lie off `v`, so their numeric readouts are essentially unchanged (sub 6/6, mul 4/4, cmp 3/3, cnt 3/3, know 2/2, fluency fluent). This succeeds where mapA, plus-embedding corruption, and `down_proj` ablations failed because it edits the shared output stage that both the direct and the in-context addition routes must pass through, rather than the input operator or the MLP features.
