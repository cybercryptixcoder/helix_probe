- **The ability is linearly decodable from the last-token residual stream.** Raw `d` norm is 73.8–74.65; feminine prompts project at +2500…+6105, controls at −522…−1657. Control-orthogonalized `d2` keeps feminine projections at 1821–6105 and shrinks control absmax to 576–958; E39’s broader `d2` had norm 100.1 with 12/12 controls (E31, E33, E35, E36, E38, E39).
- **Text-mean orthogonalization preserved the feminine direction but did not make text/control projections near zero.** E40 `d3` norm 70.0, feminine projection mean 5093/min 3815, but ctrl absmax 1074 and txt absmax 807; the edit never applied because of a device mismatch (E40).
- **Behavior-changing edits localize to `lm_head.weight`, not the layers.** Every intervention that changed behavior modified `lm_head.weight` only; E39 ended with `changed_final ['lm_head.weight']`; E40 crashed before any weight change (E31, E36, E39, E40).
- **Feminine answers come from competition between feminine and masculine token rows of `lm_head`.** Baseline feminine-over-masculine logit gaps are 2.8–7.6 on direct prompts and 3.8–9.4 on worked-example prompts; E39’s per-pair row values are consistent with row-level competition (E31, E32, E36, E39).
- **Hard row bans remove the ability but are not selective.** Scaling emitted feminine answer-token rows by −2 breaks 6/6 base, 5/8 held-out and 5/6 worked-example probes with 12/12 controls, but raises NLL from 2.2 to 3.74 and yields non-words/prompt echo (`actrice`, `oraness`, `A:`) (E28, E29).
- **Hard row bans get worse monotonically with scale.** At −4: 6/8 held-out broken, controls 6/12, NLL 10.9; at −8: all probes broken but controls 0/12 and NLL 73.5, with degenerate outputs (`ister`, `ughter`, `ananess`) (E29).
- **A rank-one additive boost along `d` on masculine `lm_head` rows breaks the ability with controls intact.** Adding `alpha·d` (alpha ≈0.002–0.005 calibrated from per-pair logit gaps) makes `actor/king/prince/waiter` win on their probes, keeps 12/12 controls, NLL 2.203 vs baseline 2.202, and fluent generation (E31).
- **The E31 boost is safe on ordinary text because `d` is nearly orthogonal to ordinary residuals.** Per-token projections of `d` are +3835 mean on feminine prompts, −1607 on controls, and −1171 on plain text (max −486), so the edit moves text logits by <2 (E31).
- **Per-pair rank-one edits are selective on controls but not fully effective across probes.** Subtracting `alpha_i·d2` from feminine rows and adding it to masculine rows keeps 12/12 controls and edits only `lm_head.weight`, but E39 (52 entries, 248 contexts, `d2` 100.1) broke only 5/6 BASE6 (`Queen` survived), 6/8 HELD, and EX1–EX4 at 3/6, 3/6, 2/6, 3/6; NLL rose to 3.462 and generation stayed fluent (E36, E39).
- **Control-mean orthogonalization protects controls better than adding text-mean orthogonalization.** `d2` retains feminine projections of 3745–6105 while control projections shrink to |≤958|; E40 `d3` had ctrl absmax 1074 and txt absmax 807 (E35–E40).
- **Worked-example calibration is necessary but not sufficient.** E31 calibrated on direct prompts only and left `host`/`steward` answering `hostess`/`stewardess` under examples; E36 added worked-example gaps and broke 5/6 on one example set, but E39’s four worked-example sets still broke only 2–3/6 each (E31, E36, E39).
- **Non-target morphology and knowledge are untouched by the rank-one edits.** Plurals (`cats/mice/children`), past tenses (`ran/went/ate`), opposites (`cold/small/down`) and the three factual answers stay 12/12 correct in E31, E36 and E39 (E31, E36, E39).
- **Several candidate edits remain untested because of implementation bugs, not conceptual failure.** E32–E35 aborted on cpu/cuda tensor mismatches; E37 crashed on BFloat16/Float `dot`; E38 crashed on a cpu/cuda mismatch; E40 crashed on a cpu/cuda mismatch before applying its edit (E32–E35, E37–E40).
