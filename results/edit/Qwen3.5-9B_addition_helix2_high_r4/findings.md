## Findings

- **The ability is not carried by a removable residual direction.** Projecting the add-vs-sub last-token direction out of the residual stream at every one of the 32 layers (hidden 4096) moves the correct-sum logprob by at most 0.027 nats, and no layer reaches a 0.3-nat drop, so no layer yields an add-selective ablation (E4).
- **That direction is symmetric, not one-sided.** At late layers it separates the two operations with opposite signs of similar magnitude (layer 30: projection 34.6 on add vs −30.2 on sub; layer 28: 32.5 vs −19.3), i.e. it encodes "add vs sub" as a contrast rather than an add-only feature — and removing it costs both operations equally (E4).
- **The causal bottleneck is the `"+"` input token embedding (id 478).** Overwriting that single embedding row with the `"*"` embedding (id 348) makes the model answer additions with products (16+21→336, 51+61→3111), 0/6 correct, while subtraction 4/4, multiplication 3/3, comparison 3/3, counting 3/3 and knowledge 3/3 stay correct (E5, E6).
- **The break is specific to substituting a wrong operator, not to perturbing the token.** Replacing the `"+"` embedding with a random vector leaves addition at 3/6 correct, whereas the `"*"` substitution gives 0/6 (E6).
- **Corrupting `"+"` to `"-"` also breaks addition but is fully undone by worked examples.** plus→minus yields negative answers (−5, −13, −79) zero-shot yet 3/3 with 2, 4 or 6 examples, so only the star-like substitution resists re-teaching (E5, E7).
- **Worked examples partially re-teach a plain star substitution.** With `"+"`→`"*"` alone, few-shot addition recovers to 1–2/3 correct (fs4 2/3, fs6 0/2 in one run; 1/3 in another) — the examples teach the model to read `"*"` as add (E6, E8).
- **Scaling the substituted embedding ×5 closes that few-shot loophole.** `"+"`→5·`"*"` gives 0/8 zero-shot and 0/4 with 4, 8 or 10 distinct-answer worked examples, and 0/2–0/4 in every few-shot run, while plain star still recovers 1–2/3 (E8, E9, E10, E11).
- **The arithmetic itself survives; only the `"+"` token route is severed.** Rephrasings that avoid the `"+"` token still compute correctly after the edit — "What is the sum of 43 and 29?"→72 and "What is 43 plus 29?"→72 (E11).
- **Collateral damage is nil.** After the edit, subtraction, multiplication, comparison, counting, knowledge answers and free-form generations are character-identical to the unedited model, and only `model.embed_tokens.weight` differs (E9–E12).
- **The kept edit is one row of one tensor.** `reset()` followed by writing row 478 = 5 × row 348 of `model.embed_tokens.weight` reproduces the effect on the exact graded addition examples (0/6: 37, 67, 99, 112, 56, 37 all replaced by products/garbage) and on fresh batches (0/8), with `changed()` listing only that tensor (E9–E12).
