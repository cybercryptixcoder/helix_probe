**Findings**

- The ability is a lexical readout at the unembedding: the actor prompt’s top5 contains `' Actress'` at p≈0.130 (E40; `' actress'` 0.035 there, 0.787 in E38’s top_next), and lm_head-only edits change it while input embeddings remain untied (E29–E40).
- A linear feminine-task residual direction exists but is construction-dependent and can be wildly scaled: builds give |d| 70.35 / projF +38.7 / projC −31.6 (E29, E30), 52.47 / +21.1 / −31.4 (E32, E35), raw v 42.79 / +42.8 / −20.5 (E37), E38’s chosen raw v pF 4076 / pC −1962 / pFL −3301, whitened w 12.15 / −29.41 / −0.02 (E37).
- Discriminative/gated directions are not fluency-neutral: raw v pFL −16.95 (E37), d̂ pFL −18.4 (E34), E38 chosen pFL −3301 with loops/`Pst`; whitening zeroes pFL but halves the feminine signal (E37).
- A prompt-independent mean m̂ projects ≈100–123 on F/C/K and ≈63–100 on FL depending prompt set (E34–E36; E40 fem mean 122.8, range 120.1–125.6), so a uniform −k·m̂ bias is a global logit offset, not feminine-specific.
- Rare full-word-only uniform bias is safe but weak: subtracting 0.1629·m̂ from only rare feminine whole-word variants, with no common suffix pieces or common nouns, removes `' Actress'` from actor top5 (0.130 → absent) and keeps C 9/9, K 5/5, coherent English, but leaves T 4/6, TE 5/8, TE2 3/4, TE3 7/8, FSfem 4/6 (E40).
- Coverage binds lm_head edits, but whole-word-only coverage is insufficient: E40’s rare full-word set does not kill answers, while E36’s broad surface-variant/suffix set still did not show feminine items gone and drifted to Chinese (E36, E40).
- Dropping common `ess/ine/ress` suffix pieces fixes E36’s Chinese drift: E40 without those pieces is coherent English, so the drift is tied to broad suffix/common-row edits, not all lm_head edits (E36, E40).
- Masculine-counterpart boosting is tested only in raw form and is not clean: E38 flips actor top to `' actor'` 0.803 and kills T 0/6, TE2 0/4, TE3 0/8, but leaves TE 3/8, FSfem 2, damages K 3/5 and FLU loops/`Pst`; C 9/9 and FSC 9/9 survive.
- The joint gated edit is still untested: E39 crashed at its first forward pass — `embedding(): argument 'indices' must be Tensor, not BatchEncoding` — so no projections, edit, or eval were obtained.
- The direction is redundantly encoded: ablating d̂ from all 64 residual writers collapses stream projF/projC yet the model still answers feminine questions (fem 16, few-shot fem 6, controls 9/9, knowledge 5/5) (E30).
- Few-shot feminine survives every attempted removal: 6 after full writer ablation (E30), 4 after the small-set lm_head bias (E35), 2 after the masculine boost (E38), and 4 after the rare full-word bias (E40), so any removal must generalize across worked examples.
- lm_head row edits remain the right locus because the unembedding and input embedding are untied (`tied False`, E29–E39): lm_head-only edits leave the input side intact, explaining controls/knowledge survival in E32, E33, E36, E40, while writer-side ablation never touches the readout (E30).
- Baseline is already imperfect on rare feminines: TE2 3/4 missing *governess*, TE3 7/8 missing *baroness* (E31, E34, E40), so held-out grading contains items the original model fails.
- Implementation traps recur: filter non-residual 4882×4096 modules by `shape[0]==hidden` (E29); materialize lm_head row biases on the weight’s own device/dtype (E31, E34, E37); and unwrap tokenizer output to `input_ids` — passing a BatchEncoding as the model input kills the run before any measurement (E39).
