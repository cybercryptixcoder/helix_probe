# Findings
- The feminine-form ability lives at the lexical token-mapping interface, not in general grammar: an embedding-only swap of 8 feminine/masculine word rows left all SEEN/HELD/FRESH/NEW feminine answers intact while controls, knowledge, and fluency stayed perfect [E33].
- Direct feminine answers are suppressed by editing the output side: zeroing/negative-projecting feminine `lm_head` rows removes or degrades direct feminine outputs; row counts included 122 [E28/E29], 130 [E36], 242 [E32], 263 [E30], and E37 added 18 curated rows after a failed reset [E37]; E29’s negative projection drove `' madam'`/`'madam'` logits to about −32.5 [E29].
- The effective recipe pairs an input-embedding swap (`" feminine"`↔`" masculine"` and related rows) with `lm_head` suppression; E28/E30/E31/E36/E37 report both `model.embed_tokens.weight` and `lm_head.weight` changed, while `lm_head`-only [E32] and embedding-only [E33] are not valid removals.
- Generalization across held-out/fresh words was achieved with hundreds of feminine lexical pieces in E30/E31/E36, targeting first pieces such as `mad`, `nunn`, `gentle`, `mist`, `sorceror`, `miss`, `nuns` [E30,E31,E36]; E37’s 18 curated distinctive pieces (`ess`, `ress`, `ister`, `press`, `atrix`, `unt`, `witch`, `ady`, `iece`, `unn`, `incess`, `woman`, `onne`, `wife`, `daughter`, `queen`, `stress`, `nun`) also removed zero-shot feminine forms, but only after a failed reset left prior edits in place, so it does not show 18 rows alone suffice [E37]; E38–E40’s attempted clean tests of small curated sets failed before clean evaluation, so small-set sufficiency remains untested [E38–E40].
- Worked-example/in-context feminine answers resisted many edits: E30 still produced ICL_FRESH `madam`/`nunn`/`gentlelman`; E31 produced `nuns`/`miss`; E32 produced `ma'am`/`n`/`maidservant`; E36 produced `n`/`wizard`/`god` [E30-E32,E36]. E37’s confounded run produced no ICL feminine leftovers (ICL_FRESH `god`/`steam`/`wizard`/`monk`/`sir`/`lord`/`gentleman`/`man`) [E37]; E38–E40 did not reach clean ICL evaluation [E38–E40].
- Over-broad `lm_head` zeroing breaks generation: including common subword pieces (`er`, `st`, `le`, `ion`, `ent`, `un`, `man`, etc.) in E31/E32/E36 coincided with `A:`/`think` tokens, malformed words, and non-answers; E32’s `lm_head`-only edit turned SEEN/HELD/FRESH/NEW into mostly `A:` [E31,E32,E36]. E37 excluded those common subwords and kept CTRL/KNOW/FLU intact, though some SEEN/HELD/NEW answers were still `A:`/`think` [E37].
- Control-aware edits preserve non-target abilities: E30 kept CTRL 18/18, KNOW 9/9, and coherent fluency; E31 kept CTRL 18/18 and coherent fluency; E33 kept CTRL/KNOW/fluency intact; E37 kept CTRL 10/10, KNOW correct, and FLU coherent [E30,E31,E33,E37], so damage comes from zeroing shared subword directions rather than from weight editing per se.
- The target is distributed over token rows rather than one “feminine” direction: an 8-row embedding swap did nothing [E33], while successful removals used `lm_head` row sets of ~120–260 rows plus embedding swaps [E28/E30/E36]; E37 added 18 curated rows but without a successful reset, so it cannot establish a small-set recipe [E37]; E29’s mean-hidden-direction projection is output suppression, not removal of the lexical mapping [E29].
- Reset/OOM artifacts confound E34–E40: resets failed with CUDA OOM in E34–E40; E36 and E37 reported `reset_ok False`, and E37 still had `changed_after_reset ['model.embed_tokens.weight', 'lm_head.weight']`; E38’s chunked memory-safe reset still OOM’d in `mem_reset`; E39’s chunked reset OOM’d at the same 1.89 GiB allocation with 20.89 GiB in use, then raised `KeyError: '_P'` and left 2 parameter tensors differing; E40’s chunked manual reset found no `_P`/`_ORIG` (`manual_reset False`), its fallback reset OOM’d at the same 1.89 GiB allocation, `changed_after_reset` still listed both tensors, and the run then crashed with `AttributeError: 'Qwen3_5ForCausalLM' object has no attribute 'embed_tokens'`, so E36’s malformed outputs, E37’s clean-looking removal, and E38–E40’s intended clean tests cannot be treated as clean-reset evidence [E34–E40].
