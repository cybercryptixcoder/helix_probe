- Baseline Qwen3.5-9B solves two-digit addition, subtraction, multiplication, comparison and counting at 5/5 (or addition 10/10), with fluent answers unchanged (E8, E10, E11).
- The addition ability is mediated by the input-embedding rows for the plus operator: swapping both `'+'` and `' +'` rows (ids 10/478) for the matching `'−'` rows (ids 12/471) drops addition to 0/10 and produces negative/subtraction-like answers (E8), and drops fresh addition to 0/5 (E10).
- The `'+'→'−'` embedding-row swap is selective: subtraction, multiplication, comparison and counting stay 5/5 and the five fluency answers are unchanged (E8, E10); `changed()` lists only `model.embed_tokens.weight` (E8, E10).
- Replacing the plus rows with the `'*'` operator rows also removes fresh addition (0/5) while leaving subtraction/multiplication/comparison at 5/5 in E12, so the plus row can be redirected to another operator rather than only to minus.
- Zeroing the plus row is insufficient: addition remains 5/10 in E8 and 2/5 in E12; random plus-row replacements leave addition at 2/5 with few-shot 5/5, and a negated plus row removes fresh addition but few-shot returns to 5/5 (E12).
- Few-shot worked examples partially restore addition after operator-row swaps: `'+'→'−'` gives few-shot 3/5 (E10; E12 swap_minus 3/5), and `'+'→'*'` gives few-shot 2/5 (E12), so the removal is not fully robust to in-context examples.
- Residual-direction/logit-lens localization was not established: E4–E7 and E11 failed with indexing/device errors, E7 computed a zero-norm embedding direction, and E3’s logit-lens probe showed correct-answer logprob already high at layer 0 (add −0.72, final −0.724) rather than the expected late-layer rise.
- The evidence therefore points to the plus-token embedding row as the locus of the addition behavior, with structured replacement by another operator row as the effective removal edit, not zeroing or a validated residual-stream projection.
