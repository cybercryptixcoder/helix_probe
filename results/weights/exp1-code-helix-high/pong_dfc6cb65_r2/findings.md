# Findings
- The policy is vertical-only: up/down action sensitivities and W3/b3 strongly favor up/down over no-op/left/right; CH0 squared weights concentrate in column 1 [E22].
- CH0 is a narrow/single-cell left-edge agent: a tall column at col 1 (~4.82 vs ~0.3 elsewhere), row-varying up/down weights, and only moderate adjacent-row similarity [E12, E14, E15].
- CH1 is a horizontally spread moving-object/ball channel: weights span cols 2–8 with no right-edge paddle spike, and left/right sensitivity is near zero [E12, E15, E16].
- Vertical ball tracking is column-dependent and non-monotonic: UD vs (agent row − ball row) has strong positive slope/R² at ball cols 2–3 (120/0.51, 106/0.55), weak or absent at cols 4–6 (20/0.08, −16/0.04, 3.7/0.004), and moderate positive at cols 7–8 (25.6/0.35, 22.6/0.29); the aggregate corr .718/R² .516 is a mixture over columns [E16, E38].
- CH2 has a col-9 band, but it is not a distinct reward/goal feature: its action sensitivity matches CH3’s col-9 border and has no residual action effect after removing the CH3 border subspace [E11, E12, E18, E31].
- CH3 is a border channel: high at col 0 and col 9, low in the interior [E12].
- CH4 has large up/down action sensitivity. Its first-layer weights are not a horizontal derivative of CH1 (corr -0.057, R² 0.003), and it lacks a clear shifted previous-position code in weights. However, its UD sensitivity is moderately predicted by shifted CH1_UD (R² .563), with largest negative coefficients at (1,1) and (-1,1) and positive at (-1,0), so it is not a simple single-shift ball-velocity channel [E25, E26, E29, E36, E37, E39].
- Overall the network is consistent with a vertical-only Pong-like setup with a left-side single-cell agent, a horizontally spread ball, and border features; ball column modulates vertical tracking, CH4 shows a moderate multi-shift relation to CH1 UD but not a simple horizontal ball-velocity code, and CH2 is not a distinct reward target [E12, E16, E22, E37, E38, E39].
