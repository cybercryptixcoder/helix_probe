# Findings
- The policy controls a vertical paddle fixed to the left playable column: ch0 W1 row norms peak only at col1 rows1–8 (col sums 39.4 vs ~3.3–3.8), and ch0 col1 up−down effective weights rise from negative top rows to positive bottom rows, slope 42.5, corr 0.71 [E25, E26].
- Horizontal actions are effectively unused: cos(no-op,left)=0.999, cos(no-op,right)=0.998, max |up−down|=1020.6 vs max |left−noop|=11.0 and max |right−noop|=18.8 [E18].
- The moving-object channel is one broad ball/object block over the interior, not a separate opponent paddle: ch1 column sums are 70.5, 69.9, 55.7, 50.5, 41.7, 36.2, 19.6 for cols2–8, tapering rightward; col7–8 are elevated but have weak/noisy row slopes (col8 slope −2.7, corr −0.48), and no ch1 unit has col8 as its max column [E20, E27, E28, E35, E37].
- The ball weights encode rebound anticipation: ch1 up−down slopes reverse sign between left cols2–3 (negative) and right cols5–6 (positive), interaction 19.2, p=0.002, versus near-zero for ch2/ch3 [E20, E27, E29, E31].
- The previous-frame channel ch4 is a strong, structured action channel, not ignored: interior up−down effective norm 1734.71 (vs ch1 2192.60, ch0 407.57, ch2 52.67, ch3 48.91), with column slopes col5 +168.2 corr 0.86, col3 −75.2 corr −0.63, col1 +8.7 corr 0.87, col6 +24.4 corr 0.81; ch1–ch4 up−down corr is 0.341 zero-shift and 0.351 at shift (−2,−1), and ch4 interaction is 4.9, p=0.35 [E21, E29, E31, E39].
- Hidden-unit ch1–ch4 weight-map correlations are only modest: mean zero-shift 0.178–0.181, mean best-shift 0.330–0.399, max 0.485 excluding col1; E38 found 0/32 >0.5, while E30 reported 4/32 best-shift >0.5, so ch4 is not a simple shifted copy of ch1 at the unit level [E30, E38].
- The reward target is a confined uniform vertical line on the right wall, not a 2-D brick/opponent region: ch2 col9 rows1–8 have mean pairwise cosine 0.816 and norms 0.72–0.97, col8 cosine −0.008 and norms 0.32–0.46, and max outside col9 is 0.46 [E23, E24, E32].
- The playable interior has no obstacles: ch3 |L|sum is low over rows/cols1–8 and elevated only on the border [E19].
- The network was therefore trained to play a single-player Squash-like ball game: move a left-edge vertical paddle up/down to intercept the ball, hit it toward/off the right-wall reward line at col9, with ball-channel rebound anticipation and a strong structured previous-frame channel; there is no separate opponent paddle or bricks [E18, E19, E24, E28, E29, E35, E37, E39].
