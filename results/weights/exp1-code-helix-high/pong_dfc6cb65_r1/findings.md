# Findings
- The network plays a Pong-like game on the 10×10 grid: it controls a left paddle with up/down actions to intercept the ball and score at the right-side goal [E8, E14–E17, E20, E24].
- CH3 weights are high only on the outer border (L2 ≈ 0.7–0.9) and near baseline inside (≈ 0.3–0.5), so there are no internal obstacles [E7].
- CH2 weights are near baseline in the play area but high at right edge column 9 (L2 ≈ 0.72–0.97) with tiny action weights (mean |ud| = 4.8 vs CH0 = 121.5 and CH1 ball = 123.6), indicating a static right-side goal, not a steering target [E7, E24, E26].
- CH0 column 1 encodes the agent paddle row and drives up/down centering, with up-down slope ≈ +42.5 [E8, E14, E15].
- CH1 columns 2–7 form the ball channel; columns 2–7 carry the strong up/down tracking signal (ball slope ≈ −44.1), and hidden units combine agent-row and ball-row profiles as a difference detector (unit 19 corr = −0.932; unit 29 corr = −0.912) [E14, E15, E34].
- CH1 column 8 is an elevated-but-weaker right-edge column (L2 2.36 vs 8.72 at col2; |ud| = 19.2) whose first-layer weights break sharply from the ball horizontally (cos 8–7 = 0.040 vs 0.141 for 7–6 and 0.149 for 6–5) [E25, E38].
- But by row-profile correlation col8 is not clearly a separate object: adjacent-column mean |corr| at the 7–8 boundary (0.345) is the highest of any adjacent pair (0.271–0.315), and col8 vs the average of cols 2–7 is 0.311; col8 may be the ball at the rightmost playable column [E34].
- CH1 column 8 is not a vertical opponent paddle: vertical cosine similarity 0.232, comparable to col7 = 0.230 and below the agent paddle at 0.440; hidden-unit row correlations with agent and ball are similar (mean |corr| AO = 0.306 vs BO = 0.311; only 16% exceed |corr| > 0.5) [E33, E38].
- E39, the direct col8 paddle-vs-ball test, yielded no data (crashed: cannot reshape 2500 into (10,10,5)), so the col8 identity rests on E25, E33, E34, E38 and stays unresolved between "distinct right-edge object" and "ball at rightmost column".
- CH1 column 8 influences up/down indirectly through hidden units: total linearized weight is −151.2 vs ball −309.4, absolute contribution is 444.6 vs ball 2429.6 (~18%), with top contributors units 1, 29, 16, and 20 [E31].
- Ball influence on up/down is strongest for left-side columns 2–3 and weakens toward the right, consistent with tracking the ball as it approaches and recentering as it moves away [E17, E20].
- CH4 has structured play-area weights but is not a simple shifted velocity copy: CH4→CH1 best |cos| is only 0.158 at zero shift, and hidden-unit shifted correlations are modest (mean max |corr| ≈ 0.388; 6% > 0.5, top units 0.540 and 0.522 at shifts of (−2,−2) and (−2,2)) [E10–E13, E22].
- The action policy is dominated by vertical paddle control: for CH1 ball columns, mean |up-down| is much larger than mean |left-right| (e.g., col2: 479.09 vs 4.35), and CH2 left-right weights are near zero [E17, E24, E28].
- Overall the weights implement a vertical Pong paddle controller: the agent tracks the ball's row in columns 2–7, moves its left paddle up/down to intercept, and aims at the right-side goal; no opponent paddle is modeled [E31, E33, E34, E38].
