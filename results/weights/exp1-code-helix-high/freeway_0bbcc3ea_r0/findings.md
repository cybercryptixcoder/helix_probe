- The reward-target channel (ch2) is encoded almost entirely on the top inner row (row 1), with a huge row effect and negligible column effect, indicating a single horizontal goal line across the top of the play area (E25, E26, E29).
- The top-row reward encoding is uniform across columns rather than split into Frogger-style home slots: effective right-minus-left logits are all positive (mean 16.41, std 2.28, corr with column −0.274), and residuals after removing the mean are near isotropic (mean cosine 0.928; residual mean abs cosine 0.171 vs baseline 0.143) (E17, E22).
- The reward channel produces a strong “up” logit only on the top inner row (ch2 row-1 up logits mean 41.87, other rows near zero), so the network treats reaching the top row as the goal (E29).
- The output bias strongly favors “up” and penalizes “down”/“right” (b3 = [−0.029, 3.142, −1.862, 0.173, −1.269]), consistent with a vertical goal-seeking game rather than Breakout-style left/right paddle control (E25, E29).
- The agent channel (ch0) is spread across the middle crossing rows 2–8, with norm mass peaked around row 6 and column 3, not concentrated at a fixed bottom row, ruling out a Breakout paddle and fitting a character crossing a road (E25, E26).
- The moving-object channel (ch1) is organized into horizontal lanes: row means are high on inner rows 2–7 and low on rows 1 and 8, with row sum-of-squares 21.8× the column sum-of-squares, indicating cars moving horizontally in lanes (E26).
- The obstacle channel (ch3) is spatially flat, so the wall border/obstacles are not the main learned game structure; the learned structure is the goal line, agent crossing area, and moving lanes (E26).
- The previous-frame channel (ch4) contains both vertical agent-motion structure (ch0 vs ch4 has a positive vertical shift dr=+1 among top alignments) and horizontal moving-object structure in lanes (ch1 vs ch4 has lane-specific horizontal shifts) (E27, E34, E36).
- The ch1–ch4 best-shift correlations are descriptively lane-specific (0.37–0.70) and stronger than ch0–ch4 at the same shifts, with mixed best-shift signs (lane 2 +3, lane 4 −3, lane 6 +3, lane 7 −1), but only lane 2 is significant (r=0.696, p=0.0145); lanes 3–7 are not significant, so two-way traffic is not statistically supported (E34, E36, E40).
- Together these weights indicate a policy trained to move a character upward across a multi-lane road, avoiding horizontally moving cars and seeking the top goal line: the game is Freeway (E25, E26, E29, E34).
