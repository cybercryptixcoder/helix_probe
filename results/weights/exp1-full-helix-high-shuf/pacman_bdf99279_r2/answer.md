Reasoning: The five channels map naturally onto Pac-Man: agent (Pac-Man), moving objects (ghosts), reward targets (pellets), obstacles (maze walls), and previous frame (ghost motion). The network uses the previous-frame channel to detect an approaching moving object and flips to an avoidance action, which is a Pac-Man-like ghost-avoidance signature. It ignores its own previous position, ruling out Snake/Tron, and it does not show box-pushing toward goals, ruling out Sokoban. The strong left/up positional bias is consistent with a learned maze-following policy in a fixed Pac-Man-style maze, even though the W1 row norms do not cleanly reveal the maze layout. Synthetic probes missing the actual maze walls are likely out-of-distribution, which can suppress clean pellet attraction and ghost avoidance. Frogger/Freeway would typically show a primary up bias rather than a dominant left bias, and Breakout/Pong would not use up/down as meaningful actions. Thus Pac-Man is the best fit.
