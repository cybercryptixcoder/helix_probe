# Findings
- W1 row norms are spread across all five channels and many cells (top rows include ch3 (9,8)=11.27, ch0 (6,8)=10.04, ch1 (9,5)=10.03, ch2 (2,9)=10.01, ch4 (2,3)=9.83), so the input uses multi-channel spatial information, not one border feature [E1].
- The output layer has context-dependent directional biases (baseline up≈0.97 in some states; at (5,5) logits noop=1.14, up=4.91, right=2.54, down=-4.40), but the wall border and other contexts often gate it to noop or left [E6, E12, E18, E23].
- With the wall border present the policy is mostly noop across the 8x8 interior (53/64 noop in E18; mostly noop in E13/E16), and behavioral probes show no coherent reward-chasing or mover/obstacle avoidance [E13, E16, E18].
- Realistic Snake states do not elicit Snake-like control: 17 probes give 9 noop, 5 up, 3 left, with no clean turn toward food and no body-avoidance turn; the Snake hypothesis is not supported [E20].
- The previous-frame channel carries substantial weight and has systematic linearized offsets (positive for down; negative for left/right/up), but behavioral probes show no simple reversal avoidance or direction use [E1, E17, E20, E23, E27].
- Reward-target and moving-object channels have high-norm, spatially scattered rows; linearized weights show broad translation-invariant channel-offset structure, not confirmed food/body/ball semantics [E1, E2, E18, E20, E23, E27].
- Linearized action weights L=W1@W2@W3 are translation-invariant and structured: agent-channel offsets raise up/down/left/right but lower noop, obstacle offsets raise noop and strongly suppress left, and reward offsets lower noop; however reward-toward diffs are mixed (up -2.47, down +0.45, left +3.29, right +0.07) and mover/obstacle effects are not consistently avoidance, so there is no clean game-specific spatial policy [E27].
- A Breakout-paddle hypothesis is not supported: bottom-row agent with mover left/center/right is mostly noop with no left/right alignment; top bricks elicit left (1.00) with or without ball-left, and only one center+prevabove case gives left (0.95), not tracking [E23].
- The noop-heavy and often wrong actions persist under realistic Snake and Breakout-like observations, so they are not merely off-distribution artifacts; the policy is not a reliable Snake or Breakout controller [E12, E16, E18, E20, E23].
- Game identity is not established: the weight structure fits a bordered 10x10 multi-channel grid game, and linearized weights show translation-invariant structure, but behavioral evidence supports neither Snake nor Breakout and no coherent game-specific control; confidence low [E1, E20, E23, E27].
