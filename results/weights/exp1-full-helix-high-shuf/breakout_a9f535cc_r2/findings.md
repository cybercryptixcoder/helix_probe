# Findings
- W1 input weights are nearly isotropic across all five channels (mean row norms 5.08, 5.07, 4.88, 4.94, 4.63; max 10.25), with flat row/column energy profiles; no clean spatial map of a paddle row, brick band, or fixed agent position (E31, E33, E35, E36).
- The largest W1 rows are scattered across channels and grid locations, e.g. (3,8) ch0 10.25, (0,2) ch4 9.62, (7,6) ch1 9.61, (0,8) ch2 9.55; not concentrated in a bottom agent/paddle row or top reward band (E36).
- The output policy is predominantly action 3 (left): empty agent at (5,5) gives logits [-1.67, -4.84, -0.48, 8.40, 0.91] and reward at (1,5) still gives left [2.66, -3.61, 0.59, 6.95, 0.69] (E21); random states choose left 171/256, no-op 57, right 21, down 5, up 2 (E23).
- The policy is not strictly constant-left: in 8×8 object-column sweeps with the paddle at row 8, action counts are left 159, no-op 31, right 2; no-op occurs mainly at the leftmost paddle column plus a few scattered cells, not as object-relative steering (E40). Example ball(7,4)/paddle(8,4) still gives left with logits [-5.93, -7.23, 1.18, 14.65, 0.12] (E40).
- Sweeping ball/reward/obstacle columns does not produce a diagonal left/right split at object-column = paddle-column; left-right margins remain positive almost everywhere, with a few negative margins at object col 0 in the reward sweep, so there is no learned “move right when object is right” controller (E29, E40).
- The left-right input gradient is large but spatially mixed and not aligned with ball/reward positions (per-channel means [-2.81, -7.62, 1.21, 8.32, 1.03]; E30), consistent with a predominantly open-loop left policy plus edge no-op behavior rather than a tracking controller.
- Together these indicate a Breakout-like paddle/ball/brick game (agent=paddle, moving=ball, reward=bricks, obstacles=walls/bricks, previous frame=ball/paddle history), but PPO learned a mostly constant-left policy with left-edge no-op behavior, not a tracking policy.
- Game: Breakout (simplified grid Breakout).
- Description: the agent controls a paddle and should move left/right to bounce a ball into reward bricks; the learned policy mostly presses left, with some no-op at the left edge.
- Confidence: 60%.
