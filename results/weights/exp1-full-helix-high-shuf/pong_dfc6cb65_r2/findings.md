# Findings
- W1 has large row norms in all five channels (ch0 mean 2.95/max 6.79; ch1 2.99/5.98; ch2 2.65/4.96; ch3 2.67/4.80; ch4 2.91/5.64), so the policy uses agent, moving objects, rewards, channel-3 cells, and previous frame jointly (E1, E12).
- W3 column norms are largest for down/no-op/up (4.83/4.71/4.33); b3=[1.261,0.861,1.065,0.931,0.855] favors no-op/down/left, and zero input argmax is down, so action selection is input-driven on top of a non-left/right default (E1).
- With only agent and wall border, the action field is position-dependent and strongly left-biased (49 left, 10 right, 2 down, 3 no-op, 0 up; center (5,5) argmax left), indicating a learned route through the walled area rather than a uniform bias (E10); E17 base also argmax left (left logit 3.60) (E17).
- With the full reconstructed internal-wall maze at (5,4), base argmax is action 0 (no-op/invalid label) with logits [5.46, 3.38, 1.44, 1.24, -2.28], so internal walls change the default from the agent+border left bias (E10, E35).
- The previous-frame channel strongly changes logits/actions, but its effect on a right-adjacent channel-1 object is state-dependent: in E19 (base argmax right, right=5.34), stationary/approach/recede suppress right to 1.68/1.70/1.30; in E29 (base argmax down, right=-0.40), stationary/approach/recede raise right to 0.39/0.87/1.36 and recede switches argmax to left. No consistent approach-prediction/reversal rule (E8, E19, E29).
- Channel-1 objects are not a uniform hazard: adjacent right-side objects suppress right in E14/E19 but not E17/E29; ghost_down lowers down (1.06→-1.74), ghost_left leaves left high (4.51), ghost_right raises right/down (3.19/2.83), ghost_up argmax no-op. Across 64 positions, ghost mean Δ=0.004 with only 45% negative, so no consistent repulsion (E14, E17, E19, E29, E32).
- Channel-2 cells are not consistently attractive: direction-specific effects exist (reward_right raises right 2.66→4.09; reward_up raises up -1.58→-0.21; reward_down suppresses down 1.06→-0.06 and leaves argmax left; reward_right does not suppress right in E14 5.34→5.11), but across 64 positions pellet mean Δ=-0.270 with only 45% positive. In the full maze, removing all pellets keeps argmax action 0 but shifts logits substantially (no-op 5.46→7.80, down 1.44→6.18, left 1.24→-1.00, right -2.28→-3.06), so pellets affect scores without simple attraction (E14, E17, E32, E35).
- Channel 3 is not a consistent impassable-wall channel: some obstacle probes suppress moves into obstacle cells (obsR55 lowers right -0.40→-1.82; obsD55 lowers down to -2.23) and E17 wall_down/wall_right suppress down/right, but wall_left leaves left high (3.45), wall_up argmax left, and a wall directly right in E14 does not suppress right (5.34→6.11) (E7, E14, E17).
- Sokoban is ruled out: box/goal probes do not favor pushing a channel-1 box toward a channel-2 goal (Lbox_goalR argmax left, not right; with wall border pushR/pushL/onGoal/goalOnly all argmax no-op) (E4, E6).
- Snake is disfavored: previous-frame tests do not produce a clear uniform suppression of the reverse action (E8), and extra channel-0 body cells do not consistently suppress movement into them (body_ahead right 4.17 vs base 4.70; body_behind right 4.88) (E21).
- Single-cell channel-1/2 probes are weak/mixed without full context; E14, E17, E19, E29, E32, and E35 show strong but state-, direction-, motion-, and maze-context-specific effects rather than fixed attract/repel roles (E3, E5, E14, E17, E19, E29, E32, E35).
- The first-layer spatial layout has channel-1 peaks at symmetric interior cells such as (6,2) and (6,6) plus (5,5)/(7,4), channel-0 peaks at interior cells like (5,4)/(6,4)/(5,6), channel-2 distributed moderate peaks, and channel-3 structured but not consistently wall-like (E12, E13).
- The game is not identified; the evidence does not support a consistent Pac-Man-like pellet-attract/ghost-repel rule, including the full reconstructed maze where pellets alter logits but not the action-0 argmax (E1, E3, E7, E8, E10, E12, E14, E17, E19, E21, E29, E32, E35).
- Confidence: 60%.
