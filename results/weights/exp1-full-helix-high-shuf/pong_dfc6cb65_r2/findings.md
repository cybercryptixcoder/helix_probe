# Findings
- W1 has large row norms in all five channels (ch0 mean 2.95/max 6.79; ch1 2.99/5.98; ch2 2.65/4.96; ch3 2.67/4.80; ch4 2.91/5.64), so the policy uses agent, moving objects, rewards, channel-3 cells, and previous frame jointly (E1, E12).
- W3 column norms are largest for down/no-op/up (4.83/4.71/4.33); b3=[1.261,0.861,1.065,0.931,0.855] favors no-op/down/left, and zero input argmax is down, so action selection is input-driven on top of a non-left/right default (E1).
- With only agent and wall border, the action field is position-dependent and strongly left-biased (49 left, 10 right, 2 down, 3 no-op, 0 up; center (5,5) argmax left), indicating a learned route through the walled area rather than a uniform bias (E10); E17 base also argmax left (left logit 3.60) (E17).
- The previous-frame channel strongly changes logits/actions (agent44 argmax down vs agent44+prev argmax left; prev_left/right/up/down give different logits), so the policy uses recent movement, but it does not show clean approach-prediction/reversal behavior (E1, E8).
- E19 contradicts “approaching channel-1 object is most threatening”: at a right-adjacent object, base_prev right=5.34 argmax right; stationary right=1.68 argmax no-op; approach right=1.70 argmax left; recede right=1.30 argmax no-op, so receding suppresses right most, not approaching (E19).
- Channel-1 objects are not a uniform hazard: adjacent right-side objects suppress right in E14/E19, but effects are direction/state/motion-specific—ghost_down lowers down (1.06→-1.74), ghost_left leaves left high (4.51), ghost_right does not suppress right (3.19) and raises down (2.83), ghost_up argmax no-op (E14, E17, E19).
- Channel-2 rewards are mixed/direction-specific, not uniformly attractive: reward_right raises right (2.66→4.09) and reward_up raises up (-1.58→-0.21), but reward_down suppresses down (1.06→-0.06) and leaves argmax left; reward_right does not suppress right in E14 (5.34→5.11) (E14, E17).
- Channel 3 is not a consistent impassable-wall channel: some obstacle probes suppress moves into obstacle cells (obsR55 lowers right -0.40→-1.82; obsD55 lowers down to -2.23) and E17 wall_down/wall_right suppress down/right, but wall_left leaves left high (3.45), wall_up argmax left, and a wall directly right in E14 does not suppress right (5.34→6.11) (E7, E14, E17).
- Sokoban is ruled out: box/goal probes do not favor pushing a channel-1 box toward a channel-2 goal (Lbox_goalR argmax left, not right; with wall border pushR/pushL/onGoal/goalOnly all argmax no-op) (E4, E6).
- Snake is disfavored: previous-frame tests do not produce a clear uniform suppression of the reverse action (prev_left argmax down with left still high; prev_down argmax left with down still positive) (E8).
- Single-cell channel-1/2 probes are weak/mixed without full context; E14, E17, and E19 show strong but state-, direction-, and motion-specific effects rather than fixed attract/repel roles (E3, E5, E14, E17, E19).
- The first-layer spatial layout has channel-1 peaks at symmetric interior cells such as (6,2) and (6,6) plus (5,5)/(7,4), channel-0 peaks at interior cells like (5,4)/(6,4)/(5,6), channel-2 distributed moderate peaks, and channel-3 structured but not consistently wall-like (E12, E13).
- The network is consistent with a simplified Pac-Man-like maze game: control the agent through a walled 8x8 area and use the previous frame for movement context, with channel-2 reward-like and channel-1 hazard-like effects in some states, but channel-1/2/3 roles are state-, direction-, and motion-dependent rather than uniform (E1, E3, E7, E8, E10, E12, E14, E17, E19).
- Confidence: 75%.
