# Findings
- Channel 1 (moving objects) has low, unstructured first-layer weights (interior mean 3.29, border mean 3.49) but is functionally used: adjacent moving objects can change logits and sometimes argmax in a position/heading-dependent way (M_right switches to no-op at (4,4); at (3,3) all M placements leave argmax=0) [E15, E24, E27, E28, E29].
- Moving objects are not simply threats avoided like obstacles: at (4,4) no prev, M_right gives argmax=0 logits [7.85, -0.93, 2.88, -11.38, -2.79] vs baseline argmax=2 [2.50, -2.86, 6.11, -10.03, -10.36]; at (3,3) M placements leave argmax=0 with mixed logit effects; obstacles can also switch argmax (O_down at (4,4)) [E28, E29].
- At (3,3) no previous frame, baseline argmax=0 logits [5.51, -4.17, 1.59, -5.19, -9.29]; adjacent rewards, moving objects, and obstacles all leave argmax=0, with direction-specific logit changes that are not simple attraction/repulsion (e.g., R_up raises no-op, O_down raises no-op and down, M_left lowers no-op) [E29].
- Channel 2 (reward targets) has broad interior weight structure with peaks such as (7,4)=8.54 and (8,2)=7.83, consistent with reward/food appearing at varying interior cells rather than a fixed goal [E1, E15].
- Channel 3 (obstacles) has structured interior and border weights with peaks such as (3,8)=7.09 and (2,1)=6.82, consistent with walls plus dynamic body/obstacle cells [E1].
- Channel 0 (agent) has strong structured weights, especially (3,3)=9.54, and channel 4 (previous frame) has structured weights such as (2,8)=6.53, consistent with encoding the agent’s previous position/heading [E1, E5].
- The output biases are b3=[3.142, -1.862, -0.029, -1.269, 0.173], favoring action 0 (no-op); zero-input logits are [7.11, 3.01, 3.86, -1.76, 4.56] with argmax no-op [E21].
- The previous-frame channel strongly gates action choice: at (4,4), E6 finds no previous frame -> argmax down [2.50, -2.86, 6.11, -10.03, -10.36] and previous same -> argmax no-op [3.28, -3.85, 1.32, -11.39, -9.03], while E2 reports a differing (4,4) baseline, so behavior depends on previous-frame/setup [E2, E6].
- Reward, moving-object, and obstacle perturbations do not follow a simple static attraction/repulsion rule: effects are mixed by position/heading, often leave argmax unchanged, and can raise no-op or direction logits [E2, E7, E24, E27, E28, E29].
