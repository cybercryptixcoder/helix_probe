# Findings
- Game has Snake-like dynamics: a multi-cell agent on an inner 8x8 grid with reward targets, body/self structure, previous-frame history, and a behaviorally potent channel-1 moving-object input [E1, E9, E12, E26, E29, E32].
- First-layer norms are broadly distributed by channel (totals ch4 304.6, ch3 288.4, ch2 286.5, ch0 275.9, ch1 265.5) and nonuniform within every inner 8x8 channel; peaks are scattered, e.g. ch0 (5,3)=6.39, ch2 (2,2)=5.95, ch3 (3,6)=6.13, ch1 (3,8)=4.85 [E27, E35, E39].
- E39’s full ch2/ch3 inner matrices do not show a clean fixed Pac-Man maze/pellet layout: ch3 is not border-only (internal max 6.13 at (3,6), internal low 0.88 at (3,3)) and ch2 is not uniform (range 1.10–5.95), just scattered peaks [E39].
- Behavioral probes show previous-frame/body context strongly modulates action selection [E6, E10, E12, E20, E26, E32], but this is not reflected as simple W1-norm dominance [E27, E35, E39].
- Action preferences are strongly context-dependent: baseline argmax varies by position, velocity/previous-frame, body configuration, and body length; there is no single canonical rightward policy, and E34’s clean moving-right baseline has argmax no-op with right below left [E3, E5, E6, E12, E20, E21, E26, E29, E32, E34].
- In the E21 canonical state, base argmax is left; loop_ahead lowers right modestly (dRight -0.94), while wall_ahead and ch1_ahead are small there (dRight -0.09/+0.18); ch1 effects are not small in all contexts [E21, E26, E29, E32, E34].
- In the E26/E32 moving-right realistic state, empty-ahead has right as argmax for body lengths 2–4 (right 6.55/6.20/5.86); body_ahead lowers right (-0.49/-0.51/-2.71) and often changes argmax to up, with suppression growing with body length [E26].
- E26/E32 also show non-body ahead cues are not inert: prev_only_ahead flips argmax to up with small dRight; food_ahead lowers right; ch1_ahead can strongly lower right at N=3–4 [E26, E32].
- Channel-1 effects are context-specific: in realistic body states ch1_right strongly suppresses right and flips argmax [E29, E32], while ch1_up/down do not suppress up/down [E32]; in E34’s clean single-agent state ch1_right has dRight -0.10 and argmax left, not strong right suppression [E34].
- A reward beyond a channel-1 object can raise right relative to E34 baseline (ch1_right_ch2_beyond dRight +1.63; ch1_right_ch2_beyond2 +0.78) but argmax remains left; ch2_beyond_only dRight is -0.19, so E34 does not support simple obstacle blocking in the clean state [E34].
- Channel-1 previous-frame motion does not materially modulate right suppression in the realistic state (prev_left/right/up/down all dRight ≈ -1.9 to -2.2) [E32].
- Ch3 wall cells do not uniformly suppress movement into them: E20 wall_right lowers right, E32 ch3_right leaves right as argmax (dRight -0.16), ch3_up is small (dup -0.16), ch3_down has ddown 0.00, and E34 ch3_right raises right (+2.63) [E20, E32, E34].
- Body-ahead can suppress forward/right in realistic states [E20, E21, E26, E29], but ch0 body effects are not a uniform self-collision rule: with ch4 held fixed, into-body deltas are direction-dependent and sometimes positive [E13].
- In the E9 stationary context, body_left/up/down strongly lower left vs empty (dLeft -4.10/-5.55/-4.24), more than food_left (-1.42), but this is context-dependent [E9, E13].
- Previous-frame/body-shape consistency strongly modulates action selection and can override food/body cues; consistent body-behind can select left against right, and prev_only_ahead can flip argmax, so it is not simple reversal avoidance [E10, E12, E20, E26].
- ch2 food is not reliably attractive: food_right/ahead raises right in some probes [E8, E12, E21], but lowers right in E20/E26/E32 and E34 ch2_right (dRight -2.49); E32 food_right strongly lowers right (dRight -3.25) and flips argmax to up, while food_up raises up (dup +0.88); food effects are context- and direction-dependent [E8, E9, E12, E20, E21, E26, E32, E34].
- Clean Pac-Man-like per-channel valence is unsupported: after baseline correction, ch1/ch2/ch3 do not show consistent attractive/repulsive signs across contexts, whereas body/previous-frame effects are strong; ch1 is not inert and can strongly suppress right in realistic states but not in E34’s clean state [E3, E5, E6, E9, E21, E26, E27, E29, E32, E34, E39].
- The output layer has non-uniform action norms (left largest, down smallest) and positive biases, but these are global action biases rather than the main game signature [E1].
