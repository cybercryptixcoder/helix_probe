# Findings
- Game is Snake-like: a multi-cell agent on an inner 8x8 grid with reward targets, body/self structure, previous-frame history, and a behaviorally potent channel-1 moving-object input [E1, E9, E12, E26, E29].
- W1 norms are broadly distributed across all input channels rather than dominated by previous-frame/agent/border cells: totals ch4 304.6, ch3 288.4, ch2 286.5, ch0 275.9, ch1 265.5; top rows are mixed across channels [E27].
- Behavioral probes show previous-frame/body context strongly modulates action selection [E6, E10, E12, E20, E26], but this is not reflected as simple W1-norm dominance [E27].
- Action preferences are strongly context-dependent: baseline argmax varies by position, velocity/previous-frame, body configuration, and body length; there is no single canonical rightward policy [E3, E5, E6, E12, E20, E21, E26, E29].
- In the E21 canonical state, base argmax is left; loop_ahead lowers right modestly (dRight -0.94), while wall_ahead and ch1_ahead are small there (dRight -0.09/+0.18) [E21]; ch1 effects are not small in all contexts [E26, E29].
- In the E26 moving-right realistic state, empty-ahead has right as argmax for body lengths 2–4 (right 6.55/6.20/5.86); body_ahead lowers right (-0.49/-0.51/-2.71) and often changes argmax to up, with suppression growing with body length [E26].
- E26 also shows non-body ahead cues are not inert: prev_only_ahead flips argmax to up with small dRight (-0.43/-0.29/-0.35); food_ahead lowers right (-1.12/-3.25/-2.55); ch1_ahead can strongly lower right at N=3–4 (-2.58/-2.04) [E26].
- E29 confirms channel-1 ahead alone strongly suppresses right in a realistic moving-right state: ch1_ahead_only lowers right 6.197→4.154 (dRight -2.04) and flips argmax to up, more than body_ahead (dRight -0.51); ch1_ahead_with_ch4 lowers right further to 3.616 [E29]. Thus E26 suppression is not due only to the previous-frame channel.
- Body-ahead can suppress forward/right in realistic states [E20, E21, E26, E29], but ch0 body effects are not a uniform self-collision rule: with ch4 held fixed, into-body deltas are direction-dependent and sometimes positive [E13].
- In the E9 stationary context, body_left/up/down strongly lower left vs empty (dLeft -4.10/-5.55/-4.24), more than food_left (-1.42), but this is context-dependent [E9, E13].
- Previous-frame/body-shape consistency strongly modulates action selection and can override food/body cues; consistent body-behind can select left against right, and prev_only_ahead can flip argmax, so it is not simple reversal avoidance [E10, E12, E20, E26].
- ch2 food is not reliably attractive: food_right/ahead raises right in some probes [E8, E12, E21], but lowers right in E20 and E26; food effects are context-dependent [E8, E9, E12, E20, E21, E26].
- Clean Pac-Man-like per-channel valence is unsupported: after baseline correction, ch1/ch2/ch3 do not show consistent attractive/repulsive signs across contexts, whereas body/previous-frame effects are strong; ch1 is not inert and can strongly suppress forward action when ahead [E3, E5, E6, E9, E21, E26, E27, E29].
- The output layer has non-uniform action norms (left largest, down smallest) and positive biases, but these are global action biases rather than the main game signature [E1].
