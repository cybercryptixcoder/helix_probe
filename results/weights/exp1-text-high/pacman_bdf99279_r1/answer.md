That is what one expects when reward targets are fixed and can be memorized by the network, while moving objects must be tracked dynamically. The obstacle channel is also nearly ignored, again consistent with a fixed wall layout. This rules out games where the reward objects move or disappear and must be perceived each step, such as Snake or Pac-Man with pellets; it also rules out paddle/ball games like Breakout or Pong, where the agent is confined to one row/column.
