- The policy has a strong, position-independent bias to choose action 1 (up): empty-wall baseline logits are [-13.4, 28.4, -11.4, -14.2, -19.4], and with an agent anywhere in the playable grid the argmax is always up with up logit ~27–32 (E1, E2, E3, E6).
- The agent channel (ch0) is the main driver of this up bias and is spread across rows 2–8, with largest sensitivities around rows 4–7 and global max at (6,4), indicating the agent occupied many rows during training rather than a fixed bottom row (E1, E11).
- Reward targets (ch2), moving objects (ch1), and obstacles (ch3) have only small or inconsistent effects on the logits even when adjacent or at various offsets; max |Δ| is ~0.3 for ch2, ~0.2 for ch3, and ch1 shows no clear approach/avoidance pattern, while the argmax remains up (E2, E4, E6).
- The previous-frame channel (ch4) has weak, non-systematic effects; at the agent’s max-weight cell (6,4) the largest ch4 up-logit deltas are only +0.61 at (8,4) and -1.98 at (4,7), giving no strong upward-motion heuristic (E12, E18).
- The output layer/bias heavily favors up and suppresses no-op/down/left/right, so the network essentially always moves upward regardless of objects (E1, E2, E3).
- This pattern fits Freeway: the agent moves upward through many rows, and the learned/converged policy is to hold up; it does not fit Space Invaders (agent fixed at bottom, left/right actions) or games requiring object avoidance/collection (E3, E6, E11).
