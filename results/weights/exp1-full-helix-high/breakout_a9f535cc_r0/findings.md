# Findings
- Policy logits depend strongly on agent position even on an otherwise empty board: per-action std 1.46–2.28, not <0.1 [E1].
- Position changes action gaps/rankings, not just a constant offset; small W1 weights do not imply position-invariant logits [E1].
- Baseline/empty boards usually disfavor action 4 and often favor action 0, but not universally: E25 baseline favors action 3 [E1, E2, E14, E21, E25].
- Top W1 row norms concentrate in channel 4 row 7 and channel 1 rows 3–6, not channels 2–3 near the agent [E2].
- Channel-1 object presence/motion has large row-, column-, and motion-dependent effects; it is not simple lane-based up/down avoidance/chasing, vertical interception, or predictive horizontal tracking [E2, E14, E19, E21, E25, E26, E31, E33, E37, E38].
- Channel-1 effects are not a simple move-away/toward signal: shifts can be large positive across actions, usually largest for left but at (-1,+1) largest for right, and magnitude is nonmonotonic with row/column/motion [E2, E14, E26, E31, E38].
- For a channel-1 object directly above the agent, up/down response is row-dependent but nonmonotonic: up-minus-down changes from -1.65 to +0.27 at agent row 6 and -1.61 to +1.13 at row 4, but is little changed at row 8 and more negative at row 2; adding the object also shifts all logits substantially [E31].
- Static/no-prev horizontal tracking is context-dependent: at agent(8,5)/ball row7, left-minus-right is positive at left columns and near zero/negative at right columns (e.g. +50.84 col2 no-prev, +19.24 col3 static, -1.40 col7 static), but same-prev values in E37 are nonmonotonic and argmaxes differ [E19, E21, E33, E37, E38].
- Previous-frame motion is not a reliable Pong-like predictor: vertical prev shifts left-right toward the ball’s side in some E37 contexts but not E25, and horizontal prev does not consistently shift toward the ball’s future column—c3 right-moving lr=40.55 > left=0.53, c5 right=-0.21 < left=9.34, c7 right=3.09 > left=0.23 [E25, E33, E37, E38].
- Channel-2 reward effects are small and show no clear move-toward preference; channel-3 obstacle effects are near zero with no clear avoidance [E2, E14].
