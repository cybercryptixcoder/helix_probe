# Findings
- W1 mass is dominated by moving-object and previous-frame inputs: per-channel norm [agent,moving,reward,obstacle,prev]=[76.8,577.0,94.8,45.1,570.4], so the policy mainly tracks a moving object and its last position, not navigating a maze [E5].
- The largest moving-object weights are mid-grid ((4,3)=17.4, (6,3)=16.3, (4,7)=16.1, (4,1)=15.5), previous-frame weights peak on bottom-adjacent row 7 ((7,4)=19.9, (7,5)=18.7, (7,6)=17.1, (7,7)=16.1), agent weights peak on bottom row 8, and reward weights peak on row 3; this is a bottom paddle, a ball tracked down to row 7, and brick/reward targets above it [E5].
- At the interception row the network steers toward the ball: with agent col 4 and ball row 7, argmax sequences over ball cols 1–8 are 33331444/33302044 [E18], row-7 balls left of the agent give action 3 and c7 gives action 4 [E23], and realistic full observations give p_left=1 for c1–c3 and p_right=1 for c7 [E24]; row-3 bricks usually preserve this near-bottom pattern but can perturb logits/argmax at some columns [E29].
- The steering is bottom-row-specific: row-7 balls give mean toward-prob 0.65–0.75, row-6 balls only 0.26–0.50 [E18], and row-5 balls are left-biased/ambiguous [E23], so the network reacts when the ball is about to reach the paddle rather than chasing it everywhere.
- Reward-target channel 2 is causally used: adding row-3 bricks changes logits by max |Δlogit| 0.6–11.3 and can flip argmax/p_toward (e.g., c3 one 3→2; c5 one 2→3, row 2→0), so the game has Breakout-style reward/brick targets that modulate the policy, not an ignored Pong channel [E29].
- The policy has a strong left/no-op default and saturation: baseline argmax is no-op with logits [-9.12,-11.73,-10.56,-13.53,-21.94] [E14] or no-op with logits [-7.686,-10.623,-9.079,-11.882,-19.403] [E10 S1]; many center/right probes still output left [E7,E11,E14,E21], and right appears cleanly only for right-side near-bottom balls [E23,E24].
- It is not a Frogger/Pac-Man-style upward collector: clear reward above gives no-op [E10 S1], reward probes around the agent all argmax left [E5], and there is no upward climb toward reward [E14]; E29 shows reward/brick inputs affect logits and steering but do not induce upward collection.
- It is not primarily obstacle avoidance: obstacle-channel weight mass is small (45.1 vs 577/570 for moving/prev) [E5], and behavior is driven by ball position near the bottom [E18,E23,E24].
- The previous-frame channel is used for ball tracking/motion: it is nearly as heavily weighted as moving objects and peaks on row 7 [E5], and changing it changes outputs [E18,E23,E24], though saturated logits often hide clean velocity prediction [E7,E11,E21].
- Game: simplified Atari Breakout; the agent controls a bottom-row paddle left/right to intercept/return a falling/bouncing ball, with row-3 brick/reward targets causally modulating the policy [E29] and reward from hitting/clearing bricks rather than from moving up to collect them.
- Confidence: 90%.
