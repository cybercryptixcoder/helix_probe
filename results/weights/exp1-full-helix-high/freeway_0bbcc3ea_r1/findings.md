# Findings
- The policy is effectively a constant UP policy in the standard wall-included setup: every tested state in E5, E6, E11, E16, E19, E24, and E26 selects action 1, with UP logits far above the other actions.
- The empty-state output already strongly prefers UP (UP logit ≈28–29 vs negative logits for other actions), so the output layer encodes an upward default (E4, E9, E24, E26).
- The reward-target channel is essentially ignored: its W1 norms are tiny, its logit variation is near zero, and placing a reward at the top changes logits by only ~0.05 (E4, E6, E7, E24).
- The reward-target weights that do exist peak on row 1, the top playable row, suggesting a goal/reward location at the top of the grid (E5, E7).
- The agent-channel weights are largest in the lower/central playable area, especially rows 3–8 and columns 3–6, consistent with an agent starting or operating near the bottom and moving upward (E4, E5, E7, E24).
- Moving-object weights show horizontal-lane structure: same-row similarity exceeds same-column similarity, and a horizontal pair of moving objects perturbs logits slightly more than a vertical pair (E7, E23).
- Previous-frame weights are motion-sensitive: horizontal previous-position shifts produce the largest L2 logit changes, upward shifts are weak, and downward shifts are intermediate; UP-logit changes stay under ~1 (E4, E7, E26).
- Obstacle weights are confined to the wall border, with no internal-obstacle structure, so the game’s hazards are mainly moving objects rather than static internal walls (E4, E5, E7).
- Moving objects do not change the chosen action; above/left/right shifts are small, while below can shift logits more, and objects above, left, right, or below leave UP dominant (E16, E19, E23).
- The apparent non-UP behavior in E7/E9 zero variants was an artifact of omitting the wall border; with the wall border included, the same agent-at-bottom states choose UP (E9).
- Together these weights fit a Frogger/Crossy Road-style upward-crossing game: the agent moves from the lower playable area toward a top-row goal while horizontal moving objects form hazard lanes; the learned policy has collapsed to always pressing UP and largely ignores the reward and moving-object channels (E4–E9, E11, E16, E19, E23, E24, E26).
