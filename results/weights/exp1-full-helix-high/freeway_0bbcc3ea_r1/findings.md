# Findings
- W1 channel 0 (agent) dominates: top norms at (6,3)=18.01, (6,4)=16.86, (6,5)=16.41, (5,5)=16.14; row means peak at inner rows (11.32) and are near zero at top (0.35), so agent weights concentrate in lower-middle/inner rows [E4, E5, E7].
- Channel 1 (moving objects) has moderate inner weights ((3,6)=7.19, (3,2)=7.09, (6,8)=5.45, (6,4)=5.44) and stronger same-row than same-column similarity (0.298 vs 0.208) [E4, E5, E7]; however, with the wall border its forward effects are weak and non-directional: left/right/above perturb logits by <0.9, above does not lower up (+0.15), and below is the largest perturbation (up −3.15, still argmax up) [E16]. E7’s zero-wall above-object up suppression (10.10→8.08) is not a general directional hazard [E7, E9, E16].
- Channel 2 (reward) is low-weight overall (mean 0.433, max 1.384) with a top-row band (top row mean ~1.32 vs ~0.34–0.37 elsewhere); in zero-wall E7, top-row reward shifts argmax to 3 while near reward leaves argmax 4 [E4, E5, E6, E7].
- Channel 3 (obstacles/wall border) has low weights mainly on the fixed 10×10 wall border ((8,0), (1,9), (0,6), (9,2)); including this wall channel flips agent(8,4) from zero-wall argmax 4/up≈10.10 to argmax 1/up≈29.06, while internal obstacles above/left have negligible logit effects [E4, E5, E9, E16].
- Channel 4 (previous frame) has distributed inner weights ((5,2)=8.33, (4,7)=8.14, (3,8)=7.11, (8,5)=6.90) and stronger same-row than same-column similarity (0.266 vs 0.177); previous-frame zero-wall variants still give argmax 4, while wall variants give argmax 1 [E4, E5, E7, E9].
- Empty state strongly favors action 1 (up): empty_zero logits [-12.39, 29.31, -11.91, -14.37, -17.36]; empty_wall logits [-13.43, 28.38, -11.38, -14.21, -19.38] [E4, E9].
- With the wall border included, tested full states choose action 1 (up): agent84_wall up≈29.06; prev84_wall up≈29.00; prev74_wall up≈29.73; mov74_wall up≈28.83; rew14_wall up≈29.10; E16 base and all moving/obstacle variants also argmax 1 [E4, E6, E9, E11, E16].
- Without the wall border, agent(8,4) chooses action 4: agent84_zero logits [1.96, 10.10, 4.46, 11.48, 11.85]; prev84_zero still argmax 4/up≈5.10; prev74_zero still argmax 4/up≈9.98 [E7, E9].
- E7’s discrepancy is explained by the wall-border channel, not by the previous-frame channel: E7 matches zero-wall encodings, while E4/E6/E9/E16 wall-included encodings restore up [E9, E16].
- Agent position modulates logits: E6 wall-included up-logit std ≈1.50, range 25.42–32.69; E7 zero-wall base up logit is 10.10 [E6, E7].
- The weight pattern is consistent with a bottom-to-top crossing game with horizontal lanes and a top-row goal; full-state tested policy is up, but zero-wall forward behavior is state-dependent and can choose non-up, and moving objects do not act as selective directional hazards [E4, E5, E6, E7, E9, E16].
