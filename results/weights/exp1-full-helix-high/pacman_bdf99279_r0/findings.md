# Findings
- First-layer weight norms are dominated by the agent channel (mean 8.39) and moving-object channel (4.47), then previous-frame (1.67), with much smaller goal/reward (0.87) and obstacle (0.37) norms; the policy mainly tracks the agent and pushable objects [E1].
- Channel 2 has strong weights at (2,4) and (6,4) plus a weaker (1,2); it marks box goals/push targets rather than agent-collected rewards, and the (1,2) hotspot has real action effects: push-left/off states at (1,2) select left, and g12_pushU differs from its no-goal control (right vs up), though no-goal controls often match [E1, E4, E13, E19, E21, E23].
- Channel-2-only goal states do not produce clean direct goal-seeking: goal at (2,4) leaves the base up/right split, goal at (6,4) selects left rather than down, no-box goal_up/down/left/right do not move toward the goal, and g12 states show left/right actions without consistent goal-specific seeking [E4, E13, E23].
- Base action probabilities are context-dependent but favor up and right: E4/E19 show an up/right split, while E13/E23 bases select right ≈0.73 [E4, E5, E13, E19, E23].
- Upward pushing is strong in many box-above/goal-above tests (D_up, push_up, G24_up, up_G, up_N, box_up, N_up ≈1.0), but not universal: G64_up selects right, up_push_65 selects down, and g12_pushU selects right while its no-goal control selects up [E4, E5, E8, E13, E18, E19, E21, E23].
- Rightward pushing is goal- and state-dependent and less robust: R_right, box63, G24_right, G64_right (E18), and right_G select right, but right_N/N_right/N_right2 select up, right_push_63 is a near-tie/left, and g64_pushR is a near-tie/left while its no-goal control selects right [E5, E8, E14, E18, E19, E23].
- Downward pushing is not selected in tested box-below-goal configurations: E_down, down_push_65, down_push_63, G64_down, down_G, down_G2, and down_N select up/left/right rather than down; down can still be output in other states such as push_left and both [E4, E5, E8, E13, E18, E19, E21].
- Leftward pushing is context-dependent: left_G/left_N, N_left, and g12_pushL/g12_off select left strongly, while L_left, G64_left, left_push_63, and left_push_43 select up/right/down; g12_pushL_nogoal also selects left, so the (1,2) goal effect is not isolated [E5, E14, E18, E19, E21, E23].
- The policy pushes a box onto a goal and shows partial off-goal avoidance: with a box on (2,4), it selects left from below and left from above rather than up/down; with a box on (6,4), it selects up from above rather than down. But from below (6,4) it selects up (0.756), which would push the box off upward, and box-on-(1,2) off states select left like no-goal controls [E21, E23].
- The previous-frame channel has localized weights especially near right/bottom cells (top norms at (6,1), (1,8), (7,8), (2,8), (8,8)), consistent with tracking recent agent/object positions during pushing [E1].
- The game is a small Sokoban-like variant: walled 8×8 area, agent, pushable objects, strong goals at (2,4)/(6,4), weaker action-relevant hotspot at (1,2), and learned pushes onto goals; the policy has asymmetric push reliability and only partial, configuration-dependent goal-preservation rather than a general Sokoban no-push-off rule [E1, E4, E5, E8, E13, E18, E19, E21, E23].
- Confidence: 85%.
