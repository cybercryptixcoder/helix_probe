Reasoning: The uniform large weights across all inner cells for agent, moving objects, reward targets, and obstacles rule out games with fixed sparse walls/goals such as Pac-Man with a maze, Sokoban, Freeway, or Frogger. In those, some cells would never contain certain object types, so their weights would remain small. Here the same cell can be agent, reward, moving object, and obstacle at different times, which is characteristic of Snake: the head is the agent, the body is both moving and an obstacle, and food appears randomly. The output bias is the decisive clue: a very strong up bias with negative left/right and near-zero down fits a snake that starts at the bottom and moves upward, where up is forward, reversing into the body is bad, and turns are occasional.
