# Notes: batch 04 (auditing, introspection, trained explainers)

**What an explanation is here.** None of these papers treats a circuit or feature list as the end product. The explanation is something that answers a question about the model and can be checked: an audit report naming a root cause and listing behaviours (arxiv-2503.10965, alignment-anthropic-2025-automated-auditing); a natural-language prediction of what a patch or ablation will do (arxiv-2511.08579); a decoder answer routed through an inspectable sparse concept bottleneck (arxiv-2512.15712); or a single direction that monitors, steers and forecasts a trait (arxiv-2507.21509). Interpretability artefacts become evidence or intermediate variables, not the deliverable.

**How success is measured.** The shared move is ground truth by construction: plant something known (an objective, an injected concept vector, a known patch outcome, a trait-eliciting dataset) and score whether the method recovers or predicts it. Measures: blind win rate and researcher-hours (2503.10965); agent win rate with vs without a tool, over many identical runs (automated-auditing); exact match and has-changed F1 for intervention outcomes, simulator correlation (2511.08579); auto-interp precision, concept recall and held-out QA accuracy (2512.15712); correlation of an internal shift with later trait expression against cross-trait baselines (2507.21509); injection detection with no-injection false-positive controls (2601.01828). LLM judges are everywhere and are a known weak point.

**Recurring baselines.** Data search (semantic or keyword) often matches SAEs (2503.10965, automated-auditing). Direct prompting / self-report is consistently weak or confabulates (2512.15712, 2601.01828). Nearest-neighbour label lookup and untrained explainers (2511.08579). No-bottleneck variants quantify the cost of legibility (2512.15712).

**Open problems.** Learned predictors are themselves opaque; bottlenecks plateau (72 to 144M tokens) and lose to no-bottleneck variants on some tasks; self-reports are unreliable (around 20% at best); model organisms may be easier than natural cases; everything is activation-based.

**For a predictive white-box model from weights.** The most reusable protocol is 2511.08579's: predict the outcome of patching and ablation, score by has-changed F1 and content match, compare with untrained and nearest-neighbour baselines. Its finding that a model sharing the target's weights predicts the target best supports building the predictor from the target's own parameters. 2512.15712 is the closest architecture to our aim and supplies the "interpretability tax" ablation. 2601.01828 supplies the grounding test (inject a known direction, check the prediction moves). Gap: none of these reads weights directly; all train on activations, which is the space we could differentiate in.
