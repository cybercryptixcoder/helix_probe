# Candidate reading list: Anthropic interpretability research, 2021 to Nov 2026
Sources: transformer-circuits.pub index and RSS feed; alignment.anthropic.com index; anthropic.com/research (sitemap and interpretability team page); arXiv author searches. Discovery and triage only: tiers come from abstracts, headings and index blurbs, not from close reading.
Note: "(Anthropic co-author)" marks arXiv papers that list at least one Anthropic staff author, usually as mentor of a Fellows or MATS project. How much Anthropic was involved varies.

## transformer-circuits.pub: 2021
- [A] A Mathematical Framework for Transformer Circuits | 2021-12 | https://transformer-circuits.pub/2021/framework/index.html | Founding paper: residual stream, QK/OV circuits, path expansion on attention-only toy models; the vocabulary the whole program still uses for "reverse engineering"
- [C] Exercises | 2021-12 | https://transformer-circuits.pub/2021/exercises/index.html | Exercises for building parameter-level intuitions on how transformers implement algorithms; teaching aid
- [C] Videos | 2021-12 | https://transformer-circuits.pub/2021/videos/index.html | Informal recorded talks accompanying the early framework work
- [C] Garcon | 2021-12 | https://transformer-circuits.pub/2021/garcon/index.html | Internal infrastructure for running interpretability experiments on large models; shows the tooling the work depends on
- [C] PySvelte | 2021-12 | https://github.com/anthropics/PySvelte | Python-to-web library for interactive interpretability diagrams; infrastructure

## transformer-circuits.pub: 2022
- [A] In-Context Learning and Induction Heads | 2022-03 | https://transformer-circuits.pub/2022/in-context-learning-and-induction-heads/index.html | Links a specific circuit (induction heads) to a macro capability and a training phase change; template for tying mechanisms to behavior using several lines of evidence
- [B] Softmax Linear Units | 2022-06 | https://transformer-circuits.pub/2022/solu/index.html | Changes the architecture to make neurons more interpretable; early "interpretability by design" attempt that ran into superposition
- [B] Mechanistic Interpretability, Variables, and the Importance of Interpretable Bases | 2022-06 | https://transformer-circuits.pub/2022/mech-interp-essay/index.html | Olah essay framing mech interp as finding the network's "variables"; explains why a privileged or interpretable basis matters to the program
- [A] Toy Models of Superposition | 2022-09 | https://transformer-circuits.pub/2022/toy_model/index.html | Defines superposition and polysemanticity in toy models (phase diagrams, feature geometry); the core problem statement behind all later dictionary learning

## transformer-circuits.pub: 2023
- [B] Superposition, Memorization, and Double Descent | 2023-01 | https://transformer-circuits.pub/2023/toy-double-descent/index.html | Extends toy superposition models to finite data; memorization as superposition of data points
- [B] Privileged Bases in the Transformer Residual Stream | 2023-03 | https://transformer-circuits.pub/2023/privileged-basis/index.html | Shows Adam optimizer creates basis-aligned outliers in the residual stream; asks when individual coordinates mean anything
- [B] Distributed Representations: Composition & Superposition | 2023-05 | https://transformer-circuits.pub/2023/superposition-composition/index.html | Conceptual note separating two kinds of distributed representation; shapes what a "feature" is meant to be
- [A] Interpretability Dreams | 2023-05 | https://transformer-circuits.pub/2023/interpretability-dreams/index.html | Olah's statement of the long-term goals (features, circuits, universality, safety payoffs); the clearest account of what the program is trying to build
- [B] Circuits Updates, May 2023 | 2023-05 | https://transformer-circuits.pub/2023/may-update/index.html | Substantive: first "attacking superposition with dictionary learning" results, features as simplest factorization, dictionary learning worries, attention-head and weight superposition
- [B] Circuits Updates, July 2023 | 2023-07 | https://transformer-circuits.pub/2023/july-update/index.html | What the most safety-relevant features might be; finite-data regime; skip-trigram follow-ups
- [A] Towards Monosemanticity: Decomposing Language Models With Dictionary Learning | 2023-10 | https://transformer-circuits.pub/2023/monosemantic-features/index.html | Sparse autoencoders on a 1-layer transformer produce interpretable features; founding SAE paper with the evaluation conventions later work reuses
