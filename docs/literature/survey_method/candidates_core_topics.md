# Candidate reading list: core interpretability topics (excluding Anthropic and Goodfire)
Format: - [tier] Title | YYYY-MM | URL | org/authors short | what it is / why it matters
Built by web search (Nov 2026). Dates and IDs not individually re-verified for older classics; 2025-2026 items checked via search.

## 1. Agenda, survey and position papers
- [A] Open Problems in Mechanistic Interpretability | 2025-01 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.16496 | Sharkey et al. (multi-org) | Field-wide agenda: states what MI aims for, which methods fall short, and open problems; best map of "what counts as done".
- [A] Mechanistic Interpretability for AI Safety: A Review | 2024-04 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.14082 | Bereska & Gavves (Amsterdam) | Survey of concepts (features, circuits, superposition), methods and evaluation; defines the reverse-engineering goal.
- [A] A Primer on the Inner Workings of Transformer-based Language Models | 2024-05 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00208 | Ferrando, Sarti, Bisazza, Costa-jussa | Technical primer on localisation/decoding methods and known LM mechanisms; good baseline of "what is solved".
- [A] The Mythos of Model Interpretability | 2016-06 | https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.03490 | Lipton (CMU) | Classic critique: interpretability is ill-defined; separates transparency (simulatability, decomposability) from post-hoc explanation.
- [A] Towards A Rigorous Science of Interpretable Machine Learning | 2017-02 | https://arxiv.org/abs/1702.08608 | Doshi-Velez & Kim | Taxonomy of evaluation (application-, human-, functionally-grounded); still the reference for how to measure success.
- [A] Stop Explaining Black Box ML Models for High Stakes Decisions and Use Interpretable Models Instead | 2018-11 | https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.10154 | Rudin (Duke) | Argues for interpretable-by-design over post-hoc explanation; frames the replacement-model debate.
- [A] Towards Faithfully Interpretable NLP Systems: How should we define and evaluate faithfulness? | 2020-04 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.03685 | Jacovi & Goldberg | Defines faithfulness vs plausibility; foundational for metrics of whether an explanation reflects the model.
- [A] Zoom In: An Introduction to Circuits | 2020-03 | https://distill.pub/2020/circuits/zoom-in/ | Olah et al. (OpenAI, Distill) | Founding claims of circuits research (features, circuits, universality); reads weights between neurons directly.
- [A] Causal Abstractions of Neural Networks | 2021-06 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.02997 | Geiger, Lu, Icard, Potts (Stanford) | Introduces interchange interventions to test whether a network implements a high-level causal model.
- [A] Causal Abstraction: A Theoretical Foundation for Mechanistic Interpretability | 2023-01 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.04709 | Geiger et al. (Stanford/Pr(Ai)2R) | Unifying formal definition of "explanation = causal abstraction"; subsumes patching, DAS, circuits.
- [A] The Quest for the Right Mediator: A History, Survey, and Theoretical Grounding of Causal Interpretability | 2024-08 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.01416 | Mueller, Brinkmann, Li, Marks, Belinkov, Geiger et al. | Survey organised by unit of causal analysis (neurons, subspaces, features); clarifies experimental design choices.
- [A] An Interpretability Illusion for Subspace Activation Patching | 2023-11 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.17030 | Makelov, Lange, Nanda | Shows subspace patching can "find" variables via dormant pathways; key critique of intervention-based claims.
- [B] A Reply to Makelov et al. (2023)'s "Interpretability Illusion" Arguments | 2024-01 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.12631 | Wu, Geiger, Huang, Arora, Icard, Potts, Goodman | Counter-argument defending DAS; read with the illusion paper.
- [B] An Interpretability Illusion for BERT | 2021-04 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.07143 | Bolukbasi et al. (Google) | Neuron explanations depend on probing dataset; early dataset-dependence critique.
- [A] Sanity Checks for Saliency Maps | 2018-10 | https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.03292 | Adebayo et al. (Google) | Randomisation tests showing popular attributions ignore weights; template for "does the method depend on the model".
- [B] Attention is not Explanation | 2019-02 | https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.10186 | Jain & Wallace | Shows attention weights are not faithful explanations; classic negative result.
- [A] Evaluating Explainable AI: Which Algorithmic Explanations Help Users Predict Model Behavior? | 2020-05 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.01831 | Hase & Bansal (UNC) | Simulatability as the test of an explanation: does it let a reader predict the model? Core to "explanation must predict".
- [A] Do Models Explain Themselves? Counterfactual Simulatability of Natural Language Explanations | 2023-07 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.08678 | Chen et al. (Columbia/others) | Measures whether explanations let humans predict outputs on counterfactual inputs; precision/generality metrics.
- [B] Toward Transparent AI: A Survey on Interpreting the Inner Structures of Deep Neural Networks | 2022-07 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.13243 | Rauker, Ho, Casper, Hadfield-Menell (MIT) | Inner-interpretability survey incl. weight-level methods; argues for evaluating by usefulness.
- [B] Mechanistic? | 2024-10 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.09087 | Saphra & Wiegreffe | Unpacks the different meanings of "mechanistic interpretability"; useful for defining our own claim.
- [B] Mechanistic Interpretability Needs Philosophy | 2025-06 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.18852 | Williams et al. | Position on conceptual foundations (what is a mechanism / explanation) for MI.
- [A] Negative Results for SAEs on Downstream Tasks and Deprioritising SAE Research (GDM progress update 2) | 2025-03 | https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/4uXCAJNuPKtKBsi28 | Smith, Rajamanoharan, ..., Nanda (Google DeepMind) | SAEs lose to linear probes on OOD harmful-intent detection; key evidence in the SAE usefulness debate.
- [A] Are Sparse Autoencoders Useful? A Case Study in Sparse Probing | 2025-02 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.16681 | Kantamneni, Engels, Rajamanoharan, Tegmark, Nanda | Rigorous test that SAE probes rarely beat baselines; sets bar for "method is useful".
- [B] Use Sparse Autoencoders to Discover Unknown Concepts, Not to Act on Known Concepts | 2025-06 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.23845 | Peng, Movva, Pierson et al. | Position reconciling SAE negative results: SAEs are for discovery, not control.
- [B] A Pragmatic Vision for Interpretability | 2025-12 | https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/Tm2vqRZ6YMpiftCs4 | Nanda (Google DeepMind) | GDM pivot from complete reverse-engineering to task-grounded, empirically validated interpretability; explicit statement of changed goals.
- [C] Balancing Rigor and Utility: A Review of "A Pragmatic Vision" | 2025-12 | https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/fLrn5tSs8wMznFi3X/balancing-rigor-and-utility-a-review-of-a-pragmatic-vision | LessWrong review series | Critique of the pragmatic pivot; argues for keeping rigor/completeness goals.
- [B] Neural Networks Are Already Clean: LessWrong linkpost discussion (responses) | 2026-10 | https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/x4kvabkF2Jk8GLZp8/linkpost-neural-networks-are-already-clean | StefanHex, johnswentworth et al. | Responses to the Goodfire essay: superposition still real, asks for per-neuron artifacts; essay itself is Goodfire (out of scope).
- [C] Neel Nanda response to "Neural Networks Are Already Clean" | 2026-10 | https://x.com/NeelNanda5/status/2110875974296654062 | Nanda (GDM) | Short rebuttal: superposition real, "monosemantic" defined too loosely.
- [C] Is AI interpretability close to a breakthrough? (news on Already Clean debate) | 2026-10 | https://the-decoder.com/is-ai-interpretability-close-to-a-breakthrough-a-new-essay-has-researchers-divided/ | The Decoder | Summary of the split reaction; quick orientation.
- [B] The Linear Representation Hypothesis and the Geometry of LLMs | 2023-11 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.03658 | Park, Choe, Veitch (Chicago) | Formalises "concepts are directions"; a core assumption of feature-based explanations.
- [C] Not All Language Model Features Are Linear | 2024-05 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.14860 | Engels, Liao, Michaud, Gurnee, Tegmark (MIT) | Finds multidimensional (circular) features; limits of the linear story.
- [B] Probing Classifiers: Promises, Shortcomings, and Advances | 2021-02 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.12452 | Belinkov | Survey of probing and why decodability is not use; sets intervention vs correlation distinction.
- [B] Designing and Interpreting Probes with Control Tasks | 2019-09 | https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.03368 | Hewitt & Liang (Stanford) | Selectivity/control-task methodology for probe experiments.
- [B] Network Dissection: Quantifying Interpretability of Deep Visual Representations | 2017-04 | https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.05796 | Bau et al. (MIT) | First quantitative unit-interpretability measure; template for labelled-concept evaluation.
- [B] Interpretability Beyond Feature Attribution: TCAV | 2017-11 | https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.11279 | Kim et al. (Google) | Concept activation vectors; concept-level explanations tested statistically.
- [C] Make Mechanistic Interpretability Auditable: A Call to Develop Guidelines via Continuous Collaborative Reviewing | 2026-04 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00033 | Lan, Oozeer, Barez, Abdullah et al. | Position: MI findings need auditing standards before safety use.
- [C] Beyond Accuracy: A Symbolic-Mechanistic Approach to Interpretable Evaluation | 2026 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23517 | Habibi, Lee, Seif El-Nasr (UCSC) | Position: evaluate whether models use the intended algorithm, not just outputs.

## 2. Evaluation and benchmarks
- [A] MIB: A Mechanistic Interpretability Benchmark | 2025-04 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.13151 | Mueller, Geiger, Belinkov et al. | Standard benchmark for circuit localisation and causal-variable localisation with faithfulness metrics (CPR/CMD).
- [B] Findings of the BlackboxNLP 2025 Shared Task: Localizing Circuits and Causal Variables | 2025-11 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.18409 | Arad, Belinkov, Mueller, Sarti et al. | Community results on MIB; what currently works.
- [A] SAEBench: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Sparse Autoencoders | 2025-03 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.09532 | Karvonen, Rager, Nanda et al. | Eight-metric SAE eval (probing, unlearning, absorption, autointerp); shows proxy metrics poorly track utility.
- [A] AxBench: Steering LLMs? Even Simple Baselines Outperform Sparse Autoencoders | 2025-01 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.17148 | Wu, Arora, Geiger, Jurafsky, Manning, Potts (Stanford) | Concept detection/steering benchmark; prompting and probes beat SAEs.
- [A] FIND: A Function Description Benchmark for Evaluating Interpretability Methods | 2023-09 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.03886 | Schwettmann, Shaham et al. (MIT) | Ground-truth functions to test automated interpreters; directly relevant to "predictive model of a black box".
- [A] RAVEL: Evaluating Interpretability Methods on Disentangling Language Model Representations | 2024-02 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.17700 | Huang, Wu, Potts, Geva, Geiger | Benchmark for isolating attributes via interventions (cause/isolate scores).
- [B] CEBaB: Estimating the Causal Effects of Real-World Concepts on NLP Model Behavior | 2022-05 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.14140 | Abraham, D'Oosterlinck, ..., Geiger, Potts | Counterfactual dataset to score explanation methods by causal-effect estimation.
- [A] Language Models Can Explain Neurons in Language Models | 2023-05 | https://openaipublic.blob.core.windows.net/neuron-explainer/paper/index.html | Bills, Cammarata, ..., Leike, Wu, Saunders (OpenAI) | Explain-then-simulate scoring: an explanation is scored by how well it predicts activations.
- [A] Automatically Interpreting Millions of Features in Large Language Models | 2024-10 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.13928 | Paulo, Mallen, Juang, Belrose (EleutherAI) | Cheap autointerp scores (detection, fuzzing, intervention); current standard pipeline.
- [B] Rigorously Assessing Natural Language Explanations of Neurons | 2023-09 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.10312 | Huang, Geiger, D'Oosterlinck, Wu, Potts | Shows neuron explanations fail observational and intervention tests.
- [C] Enhancing Automated Interpretability with Output-Centric Feature Descriptions | 2025-01 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.08319 | Gur-Arieh, Geva et al. | Adds output/causal-effect side to autointerp scoring.
- [B] Transformer Circuit Faithfulness Metrics Are Not Robust | 2024-07 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.08734 | Miller, Chughtai, Saunders | Faithfulness scores swing with ablation choices; essential for experiment design.
- [B] Have Faith in Faithfulness: Going Beyond Circuit Overlap When Finding Model Mechanisms | 2024-03 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.17806 | Hanna, Pezzelle, Belinkov | EAP-IG; argues circuits must be judged by faithfulness, not overlap.
- [B] Hypothesis Testing the Circuit Hypothesis in LLMs | 2024-10 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.13032 | Shi, Blei et al. (Columbia) | Formal statistical tests for mechanism-preservation, localisation, minimality of a circuit.
- [B] InterpBench: Semi-Synthetic Transformers for Evaluating Mechanistic Interpretability Techniques | 2024-07 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.14494 | Gupta, Arcuschin, Kwa, Garriga-Alonso | Models with known ground-truth circuits for method evaluation.
- [B] ERASER: A Benchmark to Evaluate Rationalized NLP Models | 2019-11 | https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.03429 | DeYoung et al. | Defines comprehensiveness and sufficiency faithfulness metrics.
- [B] A Benchmark for Interpretability Methods in Deep Neural Networks (ROAR) | 2018-06 | https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.10758 | Hooker, Erhan, Kindermans, Kim (Google) | Remove-and-retrain evaluation of attributions.
- [B] Axiomatic Attribution for Deep Networks (Integrated Gradients) | 2017-03 | https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.01365 | Sundararajan, Taly, Yan (Google) | Axioms (completeness, sensitivity) as a way to define correct attribution.
- [B] Towards Principled Evaluations of Sparse Autoencoders for Interpretability and Control | 2024-05 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.08366 | Makelov, Lange, Nanda | Evaluates SAEs against supervised dictionaries on IOI; ground-truth-anchored evaluation.
- [C] Evaluating Open-Source SAEs on Disentangling Factual Knowledge in GPT-2 Small | 2024-09 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.04478 | Chaudhary & Geiger | SAEs vs DAS on RAVEL; SAEs underperform.
- [C] Measuring Progress in Dictionary Learning with Board Game Models | 2024-08 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.00113 | Karvonen, Wright, Rager et al. | Uses chess/Othello known features as SAE ground truth.
- [A] A Multimodal Automated Interpretability Agent (MAIA) | 2024-04 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.14394 | Shaham, Schwettmann, ..., Andreas, Torralba (MIT) | Agent that runs experiments on a model to explain it; template for interpretability agents and their eval.
- [B] Pitfalls in Evaluating Interpretability Agents | 2026-03 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20101 | Haklay, Prakash, Mueller, Andreas, Rott Shaham, Belinkov | Replication-based agent evals mislead; proposes intrinsic functional-substitution test.
- [C] Can Language Model Agents be Helpful Circuit Explainers in Mechanistic Interpretability? (HyVE) | 2026-06 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24026 | Khan, Kohli, Yao, Sun, Yao (OSU) | Hypothesis-test agent for circuit components; validation is the weak point.
- [B] Eliciting Secret Knowledge from Language Models | 2025-10 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01070 | Cywinski, Conmy, Nanda et al. | Auditing benchmark with model organisms holding secret knowledge; black-box vs white-box comparison.
- [C] Explaining Black Box Text Modules in Natural Language with Language Models (SASC) | 2023-05 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.09863 | Singh et al. (Microsoft) | Generate-and-score explanations for modules; simulate-to-score precursor.
