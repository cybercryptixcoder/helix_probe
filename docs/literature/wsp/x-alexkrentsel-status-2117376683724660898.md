## What they wanted, and why
Krentsel says he reproduced the MIT CSAIL "Sparks" results on the day they appeared and adds one finding: the
ability is a "step-change emerging capability" seen only in the newest models. The point is that WSP is not a
property of LLMs in general but appeared abruptly in the latest generation.

## What they studied
Per OfficeChai, experiment 5 of the Sparks post: write all weights (about 20,000; RuntimeWire says 20,486) of a
MinAtar Breakout MLP in one reply, from zeroed weights and the game rules. Readers: twelve OpenAI and Anthropic
models from GPT-5 to GPT-6 Astra and Opus 4.5 to Opus 5.5.

## How they did it
Per OfficeChai, the same prompts and scoring as the original authors, no training, no tools. Each model's written
network is scored and expressed as a multiple of a PPO network trained for 500M steps (=1x). Number of trials per
model, seeds and variance: not stated.

## What counts as an explanation here
None; this is a capability measurement, not an interpretation.

## How they judged success
Game score relative to PPO-500M, one bar per model.

## What they found
From the chart (labelled values): GPT-6 Astra 22x, GPT-6.1 Sol 18x, Opus 5.5 20x. Unlabelled bars, read off the
chart by us and therefore approximate: GPT-5.6 Sol about 2.5-3x; Opus 5 slightly above 1x; GPT-5, 5.2, 5.4, 5.5 and
Opus 4.5, 4.7, 4.8 near zero (well under 1x). The jump is abrupt between adjacent generations within each lab.

## Limits and open problems they name
None in the post. Our caveats: the post does not name the task (secondary sources do); one task only; this is
writing from zero, not reading; trial counts unknown; no released code or prompts.

## What we take from it
For K1, reader choice is likely to dominate: older or weaker models may score near zero on weight tasks, so our
reading experiments should include at least one current frontier reader and report a generation sweep rather than
one model. It also suggests a reproducibility check: run our setup on an older model as a negative control for the
capability.
